Implementation
--------------

The solver groups contacts by connected dynamic-body island. Static bodies do
not join islands because solver impulses never modify them. Independent islands
can therefore prepare contacts, warm start, and run all eight velocity
iterations concurrently without changing the operation order seen by any
dynamic body.

Each island retains its original contact order. The largest point-weighted
island starts first, while the existing world-owned worker pool dynamically
claims smaller islands. Contact-cache updates remain serial and preserve their
existing order. Parallel solving begins at 4,096 prepared manifolds; one solver
worker remains the default.

Island distribution
-------------------

The fixed workload contains many islands but develops one dominant cluster:

5000,860.52,5606.97,7607
10000,1614.02,11110.00,15527

Starting the largest island first lets the other workers consume smaller
islands while it runs. The dominant island limits long-run scaling, which is why
fixed-step gains are smaller than the focused profile gains.

Behavior
--------

One, two, and four solver workers produced exact fixed-step candidate,
manifold, and contact-point counts and exact final penetration and velocity
metrics at every tested size. A direct regression also compares every body
velocity and accumulated contact impulse across two independent 2,080-contact
islands, including a second warm-started solve.

Decision
--------

Retain as an opt-in feature. Four workers exceed the gates of 15% lower solver
time and 7% lower full-step time at both target sizes. Small scenes remain
serial, fixed-step output is exact, and long-run p95/p99 do not regress.
