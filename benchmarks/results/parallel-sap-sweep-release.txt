Implementation
--------------

The sorted SAP records are divided into 16 reusable ranges per configured
worker. Each range scans its own contiguous outer-index interval and writes to
its own pair buffer. The existing world-owned worker pool schedules those
ranges dynamically.

After the sweep, range buffers are concatenated and the existing stable
counting passes restore canonical lexicographic pair order. Grid and dynamic
tree behavior are unchanged. Parallel sweeping begins at 4,096 active bounds;
one broadphase worker remains the default.

Behavior
--------

Serial and four-worker runs matched exactly at every tested size:

- candidate, manifold, and contact-point counts;
- final floor penetration;
- final maximum linear and angular speeds.

A dedicated regression also compares every generated contact and its order
between serial and parallel SAP execution.

Decision
--------

Retain as an opt-in feature. Four workers exceed the gates of 25% lower sweep
time and 5% lower full-step time at both target sizes. Small scenes retain the
serial path, and candidate order and fixed-step simulation output remain exact.
