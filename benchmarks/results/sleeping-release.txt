Protocol:                   Three sequential/interleaved runs per variant/workload
Order for each workload:    baseline-1, disabled-1, enabled-1, enabled-2, baseline-2,
                            disabled-2, disabled-3, enabled-3, baseline-3
Summary:                    Medians of per-run means; all times below are milliseconds
Notes:                      Short shared-machine measurements; non-sleeping box runs show load variation.
                            Concurrent mixed-workload benchmark additions do not change these profiling scenes.
                            Collision detection/contact geometry remain active while bodies sleep.
                            No tail-latency or paced deadline claim is made.

Workload invariants
All box runs:               512 min/max manifolds, 2048 contact points at warmup, zero reinsertions
