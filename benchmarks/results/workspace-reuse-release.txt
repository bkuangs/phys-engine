Interpretation
--------------

Reuse removes at least 93.7%-98.4% of the control's reported allocation churn.
The current mixed workload still grows its observed contact/pair high-water
marks after warmup, producing sparse capacity allocations with a maximum of
two per measured step except at 100 bodies (three).

The timing distributions overlap. This comparison supports lower allocation
churn and a lower allocation ceiling, not a measurable full-step latency gain.
Candidate/contact workloads and final scene-health values match the control.

An allocation regression separately uses unchanged workloads and requires zero
allocations after warmup for SAP, grid, tree, copied/moved worlds, post-growth
worlds, and a fully sleeping world.
